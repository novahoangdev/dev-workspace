# Design Preferences

Cross-project defaults; repository-specific design systems take precedence.

## Direction
Clean, modern, restrained. Strong typography and spacing before decoration. Clear hierarchy, responsive behavior, and accessibility by default.

## Prefer
Simple layouts, intentional whitespace, consistent spacing, limited visual noise, reusable components, meaningful motion, and complete loading/empty/error/focus/disabled states.

## Avoid
Excessive gradients/glassmorphism, decorative animation, dense hierarchy-free layouts, competing accent colors, and decoration that reduces usability.
