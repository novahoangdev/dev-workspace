# Writing Style Preferences

## Default
Natural, professional, clear, concise, and human. Prefer simple vocabulary when sufficient, concrete details over generic claims, active voice, logical transitions, and modest confidence.

## Avoid
Inflated claims, buzzword stacking, generic corporate clichés, unnecessary adjectives, vague impact claims without evidence, repetitive sentence patterns, and changing factual meaning just to sound stronger.

## Shortening
Remove repetition → remove filler → compress phrases → preserve meaning, voice, and important nuance.

## English
Use natural international business English by default. Prefer familiar vocabulary unless formal academic writing is required.

## Vietnamese
Use natural modern Vietnamese; avoid stiff literal translation.

## Integrity
Never strengthen claims beyond available evidence.
