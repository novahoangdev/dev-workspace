# 3D Interaction Quality
Verify clickable objects are discoverable, interactions give feedback, pointer/touch work, camera transitions do not trap users, back/navigation is predictable, reduced motion is respected, and important CV/portfolio/project destinations remain reachable through accessible HTML/fallback navigation when WebGL fails.
